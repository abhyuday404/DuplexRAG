How DuplexRAG works

Event-driven: every transcript chunk moves the pipeline forward

01

Retrieval controller

Turn gate: retrieval / refinement / presentation / chit-chat. Stability gate per clause: wait, retrieve or suppress.

02

Multi-intent decomposer

Self-repairs, spoken numbers, request vs context clauses, context carry-over, anaphora.

03

Retrieval & fusion

BM25 + bge-small dense, reciprocal-rank fusion, MiniLM cross-encoder rerank - on a CPU.

04

Session-aware synthesis

Cited extractive claims, claim verifier, uncertainty flags, versioned answers (v1 → v2).

Speculative retrieval runs while the user is still speaking - results are reused at end of speech unless a late entity invalidates them
Telemetry: every chunk, decision, sub-query, retrieval, version, grounding check, TTFT and cost → JSON Lines
