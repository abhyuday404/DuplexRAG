# Preferred Venue Directory 2026

## §1 About the Preferred Venue Programme
The Halcyon Labs Preferred Venue Programme is a set of venues with which the Events Desk has negotiated fixed rates and standard terms. All listed venues give Halcyon a 10% corporate discount on their published rates. The list is reviewed every year, and each venue has its own fact sheet with capacity, cancellation and catering terms.

Booking a preferred venue removes the need for a three-quote comparison (see Doc_01). Capacities below are the largest configuration; check the fact sheet for other layouts.

## §5 How to Book
Bookings at preferred venues are made through the Halcyon Labs Events Desk. Email the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example with the following details:

- the preferred date (and a backup date if possible)
- the expected headcount
- the cost centre to be charged

The Events Desk confirms availability and rate within 2 working days. Remember the 21-calendar-day booking lead time in Doc_01; shorter notice needs an Expedited Event Request form approved by the department director.
