# BYOD and Mobile Device Policy

## §1 Eligibility
Eligibility for the BYOD (bring your own device) programme at Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. is open to employees who want to use a personal phone for work. Personal phones may access email and chat after MDM enrolment. Access to other internal systems from a phone is not part of the programme and needs the GlobalConnect VPN on a company laptop.

To enrol:
1. Check that your phone runs a supported OS version and has a screen lock set.
2. Install the company MDM app from your phone's app store.
3. Sign in with SSO and approve the MFA prompt.
4. Accept the terms of use and wait for the work profile to be created.

Rooted or jailbroken phones cannot be enrolled.

## §2 Work Profile
The work profile on an enrolled personal phone keeps company data apart from your personal data. Work data lives in a separate work profile, with its own apps, storage and notifications, and it appears with a briefcase badge on the icons.

IT can wipe only the work profile. Your photos, contacts, personal apps and messages are neither visible to IT nor removed by a wipe. The MDM agent does not read your personal browsing history or location. Copy and paste from work apps to personal apps is blocked, and screenshots of work apps are disabled.

## §3 Mobile Stipend
The mobile stipend for BYOD users is INR 800 per month. It is paid with the monthly payroll to employees who have an active MDM enrolment during that month. The stipend is meant to cover the data and calling costs of work use and is taxed as per prevailing rules. If you leave the BYOD programme or your enrolment lapses for a full month, the payment stops from that month. Claims are not needed, and no bills need to be submitted.

## §4 Lost Device
For a lost or stolen BYOD phone, report the loss within 4 hours of noticing it. Call extension 4357, raise a ServiceHub ticket, or email security@halcyonlabs.example. The work profile is remotely wiped after the report, which removes corporate email, chat and any cached documents from the phone.

- Use the wipe request even if you think the phone may be recovered.
- Change your password from another device after reporting.
- Once you have a replacement phone, enrol it again to restore access.

Your personal data is not touched by the remote wipe, but you should use your own carrier and phone-locator service to protect it.
