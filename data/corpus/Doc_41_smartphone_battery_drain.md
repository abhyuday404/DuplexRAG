# Smartphone Troubleshooting - Battery Drain

## §1 Quick Checks
For smartphone battery drain, start by finding which apps are consuming power. Open Settings > Battery > Usage and look at the list for the last 24 hours. Any app using more than 15% in the background is a likely cause. Then work through these checks:

1. Restart the phone, since a stuck process can keep the processor busy.
2. Update the flagged apps from the app store.
3. Look for apps that keep the GPS or microphone active.
4. Check whether the phone has recently installed a large update that is still indexing files, which can drain the battery for a day.

## §2 Settings Fixes
For battery drain caused by phone settings, apply the changes below in order and re-test after each one.

- Enable Adaptive Battery under Settings > Battery > Adaptive Battery.
- Restrict background activity for heavy apps under Settings > Battery > Background usage limits.
- Set the screen timeout to 30 seconds under Settings > Display > Screen timeout.
- Turn off Always On Display under Settings > Display > Lock screen.

Also lower the brightness, or use auto-brightness, and turn off vibration feedback if you do not need it.

## §3 Network-Related Drain
Network-related battery drain happens when the phone works harder to hold a connection. A weak signal drains the battery quickly because the radio raises its transmit power. To reduce it:

- Prefer Wi-Fi to mobile data whenever it is available in the office or at home.
- Switch off 5G in poor-coverage areas by opening Settings > Network > Preferred network type and choosing 4G.
- Turn on airplane mode in basement floors or lifts where there is no coverage.
- Turn off Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning when you are not using them.

## §4 When to Escalate
Escalate a battery problem to IT when the settings fixes do not help. If battery health is below 80% or the phone runs hot while idle, raise a ServiceHub device ticket. You can find battery health under Settings > Battery > Battery health.

Include in the ticket:
1. The phone model and asset tag.
2. The battery health percentage.
3. A screenshot of the Battery > Usage screen.

Company phones are replaced after 2 years, so a phone that is near that age and shows low battery health is usually replaced without further diagnosis. Personal phones enrolled in BYOD are not repaired or replaced by IT.
