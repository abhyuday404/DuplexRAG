# Learning and Development Reimbursement

## §1 Annual Budget
Under the Learning and Development Reimbursement policy, every employee of Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. has a learning budget of INR 75,000 per financial year for approved courses and certifications. The financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

- The budget covers course fees, exam fees and one attempt at a certification exam.
- Unused budget does not carry forward to the next financial year.
- Books, conference tickets and travel are not covered unless the department head approves them as part of a course.

## §2 Pre-Approval
Under the Learning and Development Reimbursement policy, a course or certification must be approved before the employee enrols. Enrolling first and asking later will make the expense ineligible.

- Raise the request in PeopleHub with the course name, provider, fee and schedule.
- The learning must be relevant to the employee's current role or agreed career path, and the manager confirms this.
- HR checks the remaining budget and gives the final approval, usually within 5 working days.
- Time spent on courses during working hours should be agreed with the manager.

## §3 Reimbursement
Under the Learning and Development Reimbursement policy, approved costs are reimbursed after the employee passes the course or certification exam. The employee pays the provider first and then claims the amount.

- Submit the completion certificate and the invoice in PeopleHub within 30 days of passing.
- Claims submitted after 30 days may be rejected.
- Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll cycle, after finance verification.
- If the employee does not pass, the fee is not reimbursed, although a retake can be requested under a fresh pre-approval.

## §4 Clawback
Under the Learning and Development Reimbursement policy, reimbursed amounts are recoverable if the employee leaves the company soon after the payment.

- Repay 100% of the reimbursed amount if leaving within 12 months of reimbursement.
- Repay 50% if leaving within 24 months of reimbursement.
- The recoverable amount is deducted from full and final settlement, and any balance is payable by the employee.
- Clawback applies to resignation and to termination for misconduct. It does not apply to redundancy.
