# Travel Policy 2024

## §1 Status Notice
The Travel Policy 2024 is superseded by the Global Travel Policy (Doc_15) from 1 April 2026 and is retained for reference only.

- The rules below no longer apply to trips booked or taken on or after 1 April 2026.
- Employees should not rely on this document when planning travel, booking through TripDesk or preparing SpendWise claims.
- For trips completed before 1 April 2026, historical claims were assessed under this policy.

## §2 Booking Windows
Under the Travel Policy 2024, the minimum advance booking windows were shorter than they are today.

- Domestic flights were to be booked at least 5 days before departure.
- International flights were to be booked at least 14 days before departure.

The current windows are 7 days for domestic flights and 21 days for international flights, as set out in Doc_15 Section 3.

## §3 Late Bookings
Under the Travel Policy 2024, late bookings were handled with a light-touch approval.

- Any late booking needed only line manager approval.
- Self-booked travel was reimbursed at actuals, without comparison to a benchmark fare.
- There was no reporting of repeated late bookings to the department head.

These rules have been replaced. Under the Global Travel Policy (Doc_15 Section 5), post-travel and self-booked travel needs senior director approval and is reimbursed at the lower of the actual fare or the TripDesk benchmark fare.

## §4 Hotel Caps
Under the Travel Policy 2024, nightly hotel caps for the main metro cities were lower than the current caps.

- Pune: INR 5,500 per night.
- Bengaluru: INR 6,500 per night.
- Mumbai: INR 8,000 per night.

The current caps are in Doc_18 and are higher for these cities.

## §5 Class of Travel
Under the Travel Policy 2024, business class was available at a lower duration threshold than today.

- Business class was allowed for flights over 8 hours for Director and above.

This entitlement has been replaced. Under the current Global Travel Policy (Doc_15 Section 4), business class applies only to flights over 10 hours for VP and above, and premium economy is available for flights over 6 hours for Director level and above.
