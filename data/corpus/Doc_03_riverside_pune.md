# Riverside Conference Centre, Pune - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview and Location
Riverside Conference Centre is a purpose-built conference venue in Kalyani Nagar, Pune, and is a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02). It is about 20 minutes from Pune airport and offers on-site parking for 40 cars.

- Flagship room: Sahyadri Hall, with a full-day rate of INR 45,000.
- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount from the Preferred Venue Programme applies to published rates.
- Venue contact for Halcyon bookings goes through the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example.

## §2 Capacity and Room Layouts
Riverside Conference Centre has one main hall, one boardroom and two breakout rooms.

- Sahyadri Hall: 60 theatre, 40 classroom, 32 U-shape.
- Koyna Boardroom: 16 seats.
- Two Indrayani breakout rooms of 10 each.

The minimum booking is half a day. Room layouts are fixed at booking and can be changed on request up to 3 working days before the event, subject to venue staff availability.

## §3 Booking and Cancellation Policy
Riverside Conference Centre's booking and cancellation policy is tiered by how close to the event the cancellation is made.

- A 30% advance deposit is due at confirmation.
- Free cancellation up to 14 days before the event.
- 50% of booking value is charged if cancelled 7-13 days before the event.
- 100% is charged if cancelled less than 7 days before the event, or for a no-show.
- One free date change is allowed up to 10 days before the event.

Under Doc_01, any cancellation charge is borne by the organising department's cost centre.

## §4 Catering
Riverside Conference Centre provides in-house catering only; outside caterers are not permitted, so vendors from Doc_11 cannot be used here.

- Vegetarian package: INR 950 per head per day.
- Non-vegetarian package: INR 1,150 per head per day.
- Both packages include two tea breaks and lunch.
- Jain meals can be arranged with 48 hours notice.
- Final headcount is due 72 hours before the event.

## §5 AV, Connectivity and Accessibility
Riverside Conference Centre includes standard AV and connectivity in the room rate, and the whole building is step-free.

- 2 projectors and 4 wireless microphones.
- 500 Mbps Wi-Fi.
- Video-conferencing kit for hybrid sessions.
- Wheelchair accessible, with a ramp at the entrance and a lift to upper floors.

Because the venue is fully accessible, it is suitable for customer events that require wheelchair-accessible rooms under Doc_01.
