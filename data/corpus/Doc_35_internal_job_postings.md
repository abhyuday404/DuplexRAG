# Internal Job Postings

## §1 Eligibility
Under the Internal Job Postings policy, employees of Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. can apply for open roles in other teams before they are advertised externally. Open roles are listed on the internal jobs page in PeopleHub for at least 10 working days.

To be eligible, an employee must meet all of the following conditions.

- At least 12 months in the current role.
- Latest performance rating of "meets expectations" or above.
- Not on a performance improvement plan.

Employees who do not meet these conditions may still speak to HR about exceptional cases, such as a role change due to restructuring.

## §2 Application Process
Under the Internal Job Postings policy, an employee follows a fixed process to apply so that the current manager is never surprised.

- Inform your manager before applying. This is a courtesy conversation and the manager cannot block an eligible application.
- Apply in PeopleHub by attaching an updated profile summary and a short note on why you are interested.
- HR confirms eligibility within 3 working days.
- The hiring manager runs the interviews, and the internal candidate is given the same evaluation as external candidates.
- Application details stay confidential from other teams until an offer is made.

## §3 Transition
Under the Internal Job Postings policy, a selected employee moves to the new team in an orderly way so that current work is not disrupted. The employee is released to the new team within 60 days of selection.

- The current and hiring managers agree on the exact release date within the first week after selection.
- A handover plan is prepared and signed off in PeopleHub.
- Compensation changes, if any, take effect from the date the employee joins the new team.
- Earned leave balances and tenure are carried across without any break in service.
