# Event Planning Policy

## §1 Purpose and Scope
The Event Planning Policy sets out how Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. plans, approves, books and pays for events. It applies to all internal and customer-facing events organised by Halcyon Labs India employees, whether they are held at an external venue, a customer site or a Halcyon office.

- Internal events include trainings, town halls, team meetings and hackathons.
- Customer-facing events include workshops, roadshows and partner briefings.
- Team offsites and outings are covered separately by Doc_14.

The Events Desk (events@halcyonlabs.example) owns this policy and reviews it once a year.

## §2 Approval Thresholds
Approval for a Halcyon event depends on the total event cost, calculated across venue, catering, AV, travel and any external speaker fees.

- Total event cost up to INR 2,00,000: line manager approval.
- INR 2,00,001 to INR 10,00,000: department director approval.
- Above INR 10,00,000: approval from the VP plus Finance.

Customer-facing events additionally need Sales Operations sign-off, regardless of cost band. Approvals must be recorded before any deposit is paid to a venue.

## §3 Budget per Attendee
The Event Planning Policy sets a per-attendee budget ceiling so that event spend stays predictable. Budgets are calculated on confirmed headcount.

- Internal trainings and meetings: INR 1,800 per attendee per day, including catering.
- Customer workshops: INR 3,500 per attendee per day, including venue, catering and AV.
- Offsites follow Doc_14 and are not measured against these ceilings.

Any spend above the ceiling needs written approval from the next approval level in section 2.

## §4 Booking Lead Time
Under the Event Planning Policy, venues must be booked at least 21 calendar days before the event.

- A booking made less than 21 days ahead needs an Expedited Event Request form approved by the department director.
- Only preferred venues (listed in Doc_02) can be booked without a three-quote comparison. For any other venue, the organiser must attach three written quotes to the request.
- Bookings are made through the Events Desk, which confirms preferred-venue requests within 2 working days.

## §5 Cancellations and Liability
Cancellation charges under the Event Planning Policy are borne by the organising department's cost centre. Organisers must check the venue's cancellation terms before confirming a booking.

If an event is cancelled after the venue's free-cancellation window, the organiser files a cancellation justification within 5 working days. The justification is reviewed by the approver of the original event request.

## §6 Dietary and Accessibility Requirements
Dietary and accessibility requirements for Halcyon events are mandatory planning items, not optional extras.

- Always offer a vegetarian option at every event.
- Collect dietary needs (Jain, vegan, allergies) 7 days before the event and pass them to the venue or caterer.
- Customer events must use wheelchair-accessible rooms. Check the venue fact sheet before booking.
