# Event Safety and Insurance Requirements

## §1 Scope
The Event Safety and Insurance Requirements apply to all Halcyon-organised events at external venues. They sit alongside the Event Planning Policy (Doc_01) and the venue's own safety rules; where the two differ, the stricter requirement applies.

- The organiser named on the event request is accountable for meeting these requirements.
- The Workplace Safety and Risk teams can advise on any requirement that is unclear.
- Events held inside Halcyon offices follow the office safety plan instead.

## §2 Attendee Thresholds
Halcyon's safety requirements increase as the number of attendees grows.

- Events with more than 50 attendees need a designated safety officer and an evacuation briefing at the start of the event.
- Events with more than 100 attendees also need event liability insurance, arranged through the Risk team at least 10 days before the event.

Attendee counts include employees, customers, speakers and vendor staff on site.

## §3 Fire and Medical Preparedness
Fire and medical preparedness at Halcyon events starts with a few mandatory items.

- A first-aid kit is mandatory at every event, regardless of size.
- An ambulance tie-up is required for events above 100 attendees.
- Organisers should note the location of fire exits and extinguishers before the event and brief the safety officer where one is required.
- The venue's emergency contact number should be shared with the safety officer.

## §4 Customer Events
Customer events have additional security requirements to protect Halcyon systems and customer data.

- The customer attendee list is shared with Security 48 hours before the event.
- Guests use guest Wi-Fi only and must not be given access to Halcyon's internal network.
- Customer events must also use wheelchair-accessible rooms, as required by Doc_01.

Customer workshops follow the wider process in the Customer Workshop Playbook (Doc_13).

## §5 Incident Reporting
Incident reporting for Halcyon events is time-bound. Report any incident to Workplace Safety within 24 hours.

- Incidents include injuries, medical emergencies, fire alarms, property damage, theft and security breaches.
- The report should state what happened, who was affected and what action was taken.
- Any venue incident report should be attached to the Workplace Safety report.
