# Performance Review Cycle

## §1 Review Cycle
Under the Performance Review Cycle, Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. reviews every employee twice a year. Reviews are half-yearly, held in April and October.

- The April review covers the period from October to March, and the October review covers April to September.
- Each cycle begins with a self-assessment in PeopleHub, followed by manager feedback and a one-to-one discussion.
- Employees who join within 60 days before a cycle starts are reviewed in the next cycle.
- Goals for the next half are set and recorded during the review conversation.

## §2 Ratings
Under the Performance Review Cycle, each employee receives an overall rating on a 5-point rating scale.

- 5: Outstanding
- 4: Exceeds expectations
- 3: Meets expectations
- 2: Partially meets expectations
- 1: Does not meet expectations

The rating reflects delivery against agreed goals and demonstrated behaviours. Ratings are recorded in PeopleHub and shared with the employee after they are final.

## §3 Calibration
Under the Performance Review Cycle, proposed ratings go through calibration before they become final. Calibration is done at the department level.

- Department heads and HR business partners meet to compare ratings across teams and check for consistency and bias.
- Managers may be asked to give examples to support ratings of 4 or 5, and ratings of 1 or 2.
- A manager's proposed rating can change during calibration. Employees are told the final rating only after calibration ends.

## §4 Promotions
Under the Performance Review Cycle, promotions are decided once a year, in April, together with the annual review outcome.

- The minimum time in current level is 18 months.
- The promotion should be supported by consistent ratings of "meets expectations" or above and demonstrated readiness for the next level.
- Managers nominate candidates in PeopleHub, and the nominations are reviewed at the department calibration.
- Promotion outcomes are communicated to employees in April and are effective from 1 April.
