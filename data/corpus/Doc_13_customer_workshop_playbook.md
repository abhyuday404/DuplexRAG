# Customer Workshop Playbook

## §1 Planning Timeline
The Customer Workshop Playbook uses a countdown timeline that starts 30 days before the workshop. It is designed to fit within the 21-day venue booking lead time in Doc_01.

- T-30 days: define objectives and the invite list.
- T-21 days: book the venue.
- T-14 days: send the agenda to customers.
- T-3 days: confirm final headcount with the venue or caterer.

Sales Operations sign-off is needed before invitations go out, and the customer attendee list must reach Security 48 hours before the event (Doc_12).

## §2 Legal and NDA
The Customer Workshop Playbook requires a mutual NDA before any roadmap content is shared with customers.

- Raise the NDA through the Legal portal.
- Do not present roadmap slides until the NDA is fully signed by both parties.
- If a customer already has a master agreement with Halcyon, ask Legal whether it covers roadmap disclosure before starting a new NDA.

## §3 Agenda Templates
The Customer Workshop Playbook provides two standard agenda templates, each with suggested sessions.

- Half-day template (4 hours): welcome and objectives, product overview, one hands-on session, open discussion and next steps.
- Full-day template (7 hours): welcome and objectives, product overview, two hands-on sessions, customer use-case clinic, roadmap discussion under NDA, and wrap-up.

Both templates leave time for breaks. Adjust the session mix to the customer's goals.

## §4 Swag and Gifts
The Customer Workshop Playbook sets a swag budget of INR 600 per attendee. Swag should be branded, practical and modest.

- Gifts to customers follow the limits in Doc_25.
- Swag cost counts towards the customer workshop budget of INR 3,500 per attendee per day in Doc_01, unless finance agrees otherwise in writing.
- Order swag early so that it arrives before the event.

## §5 After the Workshop
After a customer workshop, the Customer Workshop Playbook requires a prompt follow-up.

- Send the feedback survey within 48 hours.
- Share recordings only with attendee consent.
- Log follow-up actions with Sales Operations and update the customer record.
- File any expense claims under the organising department's cost centre.
