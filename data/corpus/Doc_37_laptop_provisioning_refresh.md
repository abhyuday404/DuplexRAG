# Laptop Provisioning and Refresh

## §1 Standard Configurations
The standard laptop configurations at Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. are fixed by job family so that devices can be imaged, supported and replaced quickly.
- Engineering laptops: 32 GB RAM and 1 TB SSD.
- Business laptops: 16 GB RAM and 512 GB SSD.

Every laptop ships with full-disk encryption, the endpoint protection agent and the GlobalConnect VPN client already staged. A non-standard specification is treated as an exception and needs approval from your department head and the IT Asset Manager.

## §2 Requesting a Laptop
To request a laptop, or a replacement for one that is faulty, raise a ticket on ServiceHub under the category "Hardware > Laptop". New joiners do not need to raise anything, because their laptop is provisioned automatically once HR confirms the joining date.

1. Open ServiceHub and choose "New Request".
2. Select "Hardware > Laptop" and state whether you need an engineering or a business configuration.
3. Add your manager as approver and your preferred collection location.
4. Submit the ticket and track it from "My Requests".

Devices are normally ready for collection within 3 working days of approval.

## §3 Refresh Cycle
The laptop refresh cycle at Halcyon Labs is every 4 years, counted from the date of issue recorded in the asset register. IT sends a ServiceHub notification 60 days before your device is due.

An early refresh is approved when the repair cost exceeds 40% of the device value. IT Support prepares a repair estimate, compares it with the current book value of the laptop and closes the ticket as a replacement when the threshold is crossed. Returned devices are wiped to the company standard before recycling or resale.

## §4 Loss or Damage
For a lost, stolen or damaged laptop, report the incident to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours by raising a ServiceHub ticket or calling extension 4357.

- Theft: a police FIR is required, and a copy must be attached to the ServiceHub ticket.
- Loss: file the report first, then follow the steps in the Security Incident Reporting policy so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.
- Damage: bring the device to the IT desk so that the damage can be assessed.

Where the loss or damage is found to result from negligence, such as leaving a laptop unattended in a vehicle, an INR 10,000 deductible is payable by the employee. Genuine accidents and theft with a valid FIR are not charged the deductible unless negligence is established.
