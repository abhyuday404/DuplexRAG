# Cubbon Heritage Hall, Bengaluru - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
Cubbon Heritage Hall is a heritage building near Cubbon Park and MG Road in central Bengaluru, and a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02). It offers parking for only 15 cars, so organisers should encourage attendees to use the nearby MG Road metro station or cab pooling.

- The building is well suited to leadership meetings, customer receptions and evening events.
- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount applies to published rates.
- Bookings go through the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example.

## §2 Capacity
Cubbon Heritage Hall has one main indoor hall and an outdoor terrace.

- Main Hall: 80 theatre or 50 classroom.
- Garden Terrace: 60 standing, for evening events.

The Garden Terrace is available for evening use only and is subject to weather; the Main Hall is the fallback space and must be reserved together with it.

## §3 Booking and Cancellation Policy
Cubbon Heritage Hall requires a large advance and has a long free-cancellation window.

- 50% advance payment at booking.
- Free cancellation up to 21 days before the event.
- 50% charge if cancelled 8-20 days before.
- 100% charge within 7 days.

Organisers should confirm the booking only after approvals are complete under Doc_01, since the advance is paid at the time of booking.

## §4 Catering
Catering at Cubbon Heritage Hall is only through the two partner caterers listed in Doc_11: Tiffin Trail and Malnad Kitchens.

- Expect roughly INR 1,100 per head.
- The venue has no in-house kitchen, and other caterers are not permitted.
- Orders are placed by the organiser directly with the partner caterer, following the ordering rules in Doc_11.

## §5 House Rules
The house rules at Cubbon Heritage Hall exist to protect the heritage structure and neighbouring residents.

- No amplified music after 10 pm.
- No nails or tape on heritage walls. Signage must use free-standing stands.
- Organisers are responsible for any damage caused by their contractors or attendees.
