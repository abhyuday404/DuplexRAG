# HITEC City Convention Rooms, Hyderabad - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
HITEC City Convention Rooms is a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02) in the HITEC City business district of Hyderabad. It is 45 minutes from the airport by road, so out-of-town attendees should allow extra travel time on arrival and departure days.

- It is the largest preferred venue in the Halcyon network, and the first choice for regional conferences.
- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount applies to published rates.
- Bookings go through the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example.

## §2 Capacity
HITEC City Convention Rooms has one large room and four smaller rooms.

- Grand Room: 150 theatre or 90 classroom.
- Four breakout rooms of 12 each.

The breakout rooms are adjacent to the Grand Room, which makes them convenient for parallel sessions during customer workshops and for small-group discussions during all-hands events.

## §3 Cancellation Policy
The cancellation policy at HITEC City Convention Rooms has two stages.

- Free cancellation until 14 days before the event.
- A 50% charge applies to any cancellation after that.

Under Doc_01, cancellation charges are borne by the organising department's cost centre, and a justification is due within 5 working days if the free window has passed.

## §4 Catering
HITEC City Convention Rooms allows in-house catering only, and outside caterers are not permitted.

- Hyderabadi menu: INR 1,050 per head.
- Vegetarian menu: INR 900 per head.

Under Doc_01, a vegetarian option must always be offered, and dietary needs such as Jain, vegan and allergies should be collected 7 days before the event and passed to the venue.

## §5 AV and Accessibility
HITEC City Convention Rooms has AV suited to large hybrid events.

- Dual LED walls in the Grand Room.
- A simultaneous interpretation booth, useful for multilingual customer events.
- Wheelchair accessible.

Because the venue is wheelchair accessible, it meets the accessibility requirement for customer events in Doc_01.
