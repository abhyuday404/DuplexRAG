# Global Travel Policy

## §1 Scope and Applicability
The Global Travel Policy applies to all employees and contractors of Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. on business travel, within India or abroad. It covers flights, trains and hotels arranged through the corporate travel desk, TripDesk.

- The policy supersedes the Travel Policy 2024 (Doc_23) from 1 April 2026.
- Contractors are bound by it, and their travel is charged to the sponsoring department.

## §2 Booking Channel
Under the Global Travel Policy, all flights and hotels must be booked through TripDesk, which provides negotiated fares and automatic policy checks.

- Self-booked travel is reimbursed only through the exception process in section 5 of this policy.
- TripDesk records the benchmark fare for each route at the time of search, and this fare is used if a booking is later reviewed.

## §3 Advance Booking Windows
Under the Global Travel Policy, travel must be booked ahead of departure by the following minimum windows.

- Domestic flights: at least 7 days before departure.
- International flights: at least 21 days before departure.
- Train travel: at least 5 days before departure.

Bookings made inside these windows are late bookings and follow the approval rules in section 5.

## §4 Class of Travel
Under the Global Travel Policy, the class of air travel depends on flight duration and the traveller's grade.

- Economy class applies to all flights up to 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours for Director level and above.
- Business class is allowed for flights over 10 hours for VP and above only.

Flight duration is the scheduled flying time of the longest single leg shown on TripDesk. Employees may pay the difference personally to travel in a higher class.

## §5 Late Booking and Exceptions
Under the Global Travel Policy, late bookings and bookings made outside TripDesk are handled through the exception process.

- A booking made inside the advance window needs line manager approval with a business justification.
- A booking made after the travel has taken place (a post-travel or retroactive booking, or travel self-booked outside TripDesk) requires approval from a senior director (L8 or above).
- Such after-the-fact bookings are reimbursed at the lower of the actual fare or the TripDesk benchmark fare.
- Three or more late bookings in a quarter are reported to the department head.

## §6 International Travel Requirements
Under the Global Travel Policy, international trips carry additional requirements that must be met before departure.

- Pre-trip approval from the department director is required.
- Travel insurance is mandatory (see Doc_20).
- Forex may be obtained only through the TripDesk forex card.
- Every foreign-currency expense must be supported by an original receipt and goes through foreign currency receipt verification (Doc_16 Section 4).

Visa applications should start at least 30 days before travel, as described in Doc_20.
