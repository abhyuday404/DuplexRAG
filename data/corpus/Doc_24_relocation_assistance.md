# Relocation Assistance

## §1 Eligibility
Under the Relocation Assistance policy, relocation support is available to two groups of employees who move to a new city for work.

- Employees whose inter-city transfer is initiated by the company, with a transfer letter issued through PeopleHub.
- New hires who are relocating to the work city on the basis of their offer letter.

Employees who request a transfer for personal reasons are not covered unless the business head approves it as company-initiated. The new work location must be at least 100 km from the current residence.

## §2 Moving Allowance
Under the Relocation Assistance policy, an eligible employee receives a moving allowance to cover the cost of shifting the household.

- Household goods shipment is covered up to INR 1,50,000, paid against invoices from a packer and mover who is on the company's approved vendor list.
- One-way airfare is provided for the employee's family (spouse and dependent children) to the new city, booked through TripDesk under the Global Travel Policy (Doc_15).
- Insurance for goods in transit is reimbursed within the same limit.

Claims for the moving allowance are submitted in SpendWise with original invoices within 30 days of joining the new location.

## §3 Temporary Housing
Under the Relocation Assistance policy, the company provides temporary housing while the employee finds a permanent residence.

- Temporary housing is available for up to 30 days in a company-paid serviced apartment arranged through TripDesk.
- The apartment is booked at the rate applicable to the city as per the hotel caps in Doc_18.
- Stays beyond 30 days are at the employee's own cost unless the business head grants an extension in writing.

Per diem is not payable during temporary housing, but the employee may claim ground transport to the work location as per Doc_19.

## §4 Clawback
Under the Relocation Assistance policy, relocation benefits are subject to a clawback if the employee leaves the company soon after the move.

- An employee who resigns or is terminated for cause within 12 months of relocation must repay the benefits pro-rata.
- The repayment is calculated on the number of months remaining out of the 12-month period.
- The amount is recovered from the full and final settlement, and any shortfall is payable within 30 days.

Employees sign the relocation agreement acknowledging this clause before any benefit is paid.
