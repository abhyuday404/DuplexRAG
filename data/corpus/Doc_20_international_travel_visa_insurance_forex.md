# International Travel: Visa, Insurance and Forex

## §1 Visa
Under the International Travel policy, visa applications for business trips must be started at least 30 days before the date of travel.

- Applications are made through the TripDesk visa desk, which prepares the documents, books appointments and tracks the status.
- Visa fees are reimbursed in full, together with courier and biometric charges levied by the consulate.
- Employees must check that the passport has at least 6 months of validity and two blank pages before applying.

Trips that need an urgent visa require director approval, and expedited-processing charges are reimbursed only with that approval.

## §2 Travel Insurance
Under the International Travel policy, travel insurance is mandatory for every international trip, and no traveller may depart without cover.

- The group policy is HLI-GTI-2026, administered by the Risk team.
- Medical cover is up to USD 100,000 per traveller per trip.
- The policy is Schengen compliant, and the insurance certificate can be downloaded from TripDesk for visa applications.
- Cover is activated automatically when the trip is booked through TripDesk.

Insurance is a condition of the Global Travel Policy (Doc_15 Section 6). Medical emergencies abroad are handled as described in Doc_26.

## §3 Forex
Under the International Travel policy, foreign currency for business trips is obtained only through the TripDesk forex card.

- The forex card is loaded up to USD 300 per day of travel.
- Cash withdrawals are limited to USD 500 per trip.
- Unused forex must be returned within 7 days of return from the trip, and the card balance is settled with Finance.

Loading requests are raised in TripDesk after the trip is approved, and the card is usually ready within 3 working days. Forex bought from any other source is not reimbursed.

## §4 Receipts Abroad
Under the International Travel policy, employees must keep original receipts for every expense incurred abroad.

- Photograph each receipt in the SpendWise app on the same day, while the details are still fresh.
- Keep the original paper receipts until the claim is paid.
- Finance performs foreign currency receipt verification per Doc_16 Section 4, and converts amounts at the forex card rate or, for card and cash spends, at the RBI reference rate on the date of the expense.

Receipts must show the amount and the currency. Without verifiable receipts, incidentals are capped at USD 25 per day.
