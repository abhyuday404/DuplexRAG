# Code of Conduct and POSH

## §1 Principles
Under the Code of Conduct, all employees, contractors and interns of Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. are expected to act on three core principles: integrity, respect and compliance with law.

- Integrity: be honest in dealings with colleagues, customers and partners, and keep accurate records.
- Respect: treat everyone fairly and without discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, disability, age or any other personal characteristic.
- Compliance with law: follow all applicable Indian laws, regulations and company policies.

Managers are expected to model these principles and address concerns promptly.

## §2 Conflicts of Interest
Under the Code of Conduct, a conflict of interest arises when a personal interest could influence, or appear to influence, a business decision. Examples include a family member working for a vendor, or a paid side engagement with a competitor.

- Declare the conflict within 30 days of the conflict arising.
- Submit the declaration to your manager and HR through PeopleHub.
- The company may approve the situation with safeguards, reassign duties, or ask that the interest be ended.

## §3 POSH
Under the Code of Conduct, the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) framework protects everyone at work, including visitors and on business travel. The Internal Committee (IC) handles sexual harassment complaints.

- A complaint should be made within 3 months of the incident. The IC can extend this period in genuine cases.
- Email the IC at ic@halcyonlabs.example.
- The IC is led by a senior woman member and includes an external member.
- Proceedings are confidential, and the parties are heard fairly.
- Interim relief such as a change in reporting line may be provided during the inquiry.

## §4 Speak-Up and Whistleblowing
Under the Code of Conduct, employees are encouraged to raise concerns about fraud, bribery, data misuse, safety or any breach of policy. Halcyon Labs runs an anonymous ethics hotline, available 24 hours a day and operated by an independent provider.

- Reporters may stay anonymous and can track their report through a case number.
- There is no retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Retaliation is itself a disciplinary matter.
- All reports are reviewed by the Ethics Officer and closed with a written outcome.
