# Per Diem and Meal Allowances

## §1 How Per Diem Works
Under the Per Diem and Meal Allowances policy, the per diem is a fixed daily amount that covers meals and incidentals for employees on business travel.

- No receipts are needed for per diem; the amount is claimed as a flat daily rate in SpendWise.
- Per diem is not payable for any meal that is provided by an event, a client or a host, such as a conference lunch or a client dinner. The claim for that day is reduced accordingly.
- Hotel breakfast included in the room rate counts as a provided meal.
- Per diem is paid in addition to the hotel caps in Doc_18 and the local transport rules in Doc_19.

## §2 Domestic City Tiers
Under the Per Diem and Meal Allowances policy, domestic per diem depends on the tier of the city being visited.

- Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai): INR 2,500 per day.
- Tier 2 cities: INR 1,800 per day.
- Tier 3 cities: INR 1,200 per day.

Any city not named in the Tier 1 list is classified as Tier 2 or Tier 3 by Finance, and the current classification is published on PeopleHub. The rate for the city where the traveller spends the night applies for that day.

## §3 International Per Diem
Under the Per Diem and Meal Allowances policy, international per diem is paid in the local currency of the destination country at the following daily rates.

- USA: USD 75 per day.
- UK: GBP 60 per day.
- Germany: EUR 65 per day.
- Singapore: SGD 90 per day.
- Japan: JPY 9,000 per day.
- All other countries: USD 60 per day.

International per diem does not require receipts. Incidental expenses beyond the per diem must be supported by original receipts under foreign currency receipt verification (Doc_16 Section 4).

## §4 Partial Days
Under the Per Diem and Meal Allowances policy, days on which the employee is only part of the day at the destination are paid at a reduced rate.

- Travel days (the day of departure and the day of return) are paid at 75% of the applicable per diem.
- Day trips of less than 8 hours, where the employee returns home the same day, are paid at 50% of the applicable per diem.
- A day trip of 8 hours or more is paid at the full rate for the destination city tier.

Example: a two-night trip to Pune has one full day and two travel days, so the per diem is 2.5 times the Tier 1 rate, which is INR 6,250.
