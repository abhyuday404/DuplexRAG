# Sector 62 Learning Centre, Noida - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
Sector 62 Learning Centre is a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02) in Sector 62, Noida, near the Electronic City metro station. The metro link makes it easy to reach from Delhi and Gurugram, so it works well for trainings that draw attendees from across the National Capital Region.

- It is a training-focused venue with two classrooms.
- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount applies to published rates.
- Bookings go through the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example.

## §2 Capacity
Sector 62 Learning Centre has two training rooms.

- Training Room 1: 50 classroom.
- Training Room 2: 20 classroom.

The rooms are separate and are not combined into a single space. For a training of more than 50 people, organisers should look at another venue in Doc_02.

## §3 Booking and Cancellation Policy
The booking and cancellation policy at Sector 62 Learning Centre is based on working days rather than calendar days.

- Free cancellation until 5 working days before the event.
- A 20% fee applies to cancellations after that.
- Weekend bookings need 10 days notice.

Under the Event Planning Policy (Doc_01), any charge is borne by the organising department's cost centre, and a cancellation justification is due within 5 working days if the free window has passed.

## §4 AV and Access
Sector 62 Learning Centre has basic AV and reliable building infrastructure.

- Both rooms are on the ground floor, with step-free access from the entrance.
- 8-hour power backup, so sessions continue through most local outages.
- Visitors need a government photo ID for passes at the gate, so organisers should remind external participants to carry one.
- Each room has a projector and a whiteboard.

For customer events, the attendee list should also be shared with Halcyon Security in line with Doc_12.
