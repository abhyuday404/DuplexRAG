# Hinjewadi Tech Park Training Suites, Pune - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview and Location
Hinjewadi Tech Park Training Suites is a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02) in Hinjewadi Phase 1, Pune, about 5 minutes from the Halcyon Pune office. It is well suited to hands-on technical trainings, certification prep and onboarding batches.

- Suite A full-day rate: INR 18,000.
- Suite B full-day rate: INR 24,000.
- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount applies to published rates.
- Standard operating hours for a full-day booking are 9 am to 6 pm.

## §2 Capacity and Layouts
Hinjewadi Tech Park Training Suites has two adjoining suites that can be used separately or joined.

- Suite A: 24 classroom.
- Suite B: 32 classroom, with a 30-workstation lab setup.
- Suites A and B combine into one room of 56 classroom, by opening the folding partition between them.

The Suite B lab workstations are pre-imaged with a standard Windows and Linux developer environment. Requests for custom images must reach the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example at least 7 days before the event.

## §3 Booking and Cancellation Policy
The cancellation policy at Hinjewadi Tech Park Training Suites is simple and has three stages.

- Free cancellation until 7 days before the event.
- A 25% fee applies to cancellations made after that point.
- A no-show is charged 100%.

Under the Event Planning Policy (Doc_01), any charge is borne by the organising department's cost centre, and a cancellation justification is due within 5 working days if the free window has passed.

## §4 Facilities, AV and Accessibility
Hinjewadi Tech Park Training Suites provides a technical training environment with the following facilities.

- Interactive displays in both suites.
- 1 Gbps wired network at every workstation in the Suite B lab.
- Lockers for participants' laptops and personal items, available at the reception.
- On-site technical support during booked hours.

Both suites are on the ground floor and are wheelchair accessible, with a level entrance from the park driveway. The venue is therefore suitable for customer events that require accessible rooms.
