# Meeting Room AV and Video Conferencing

## §1 Booking Rooms
Meeting room booking at Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. is done through the company calendar. Add the room as a resource when you create the invite, and check its capacity and equipment in the room description.

- Recurring bookings are limited to 2 hours per occurrence.
- Release a room you no longer need. Rooms are released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes of the start time.
- Use the touch panel outside the room for same-day ad hoc bookings.

If you need a room for a longer working session, split the booking into separate slots or ask the facilities team for a project room.

## §2 Hybrid Rooms
The hybrid meeting rooms are the ones tagged "H" in the calendar. Rooms tagged "H" have a 360-degree camera and ceiling microphones, so remote participants can see and hear everyone in the room.

1. Start the meeting from the room's touch panel or join the invite from the room display.
2. Select the room camera and the ceiling microphones as your devices.
3. Sit within 5 metres of the camera for the best pickup.

The camera tracks the active speaker automatically, and you can switch to a wide view from the touch panel. Rooms without the "H" tag have a standard camera and a single tabletop microphone.

## §3 Troubleshooting
For common problems with meeting room AV, try these fixes first.

- No display: press Input on the touch panel and select the cable or wireless option that matches your laptop, then check that the cable is firmly seated.
- Echo: mute the laptop microphone when using the room mic. Echo appears when both your laptop and the room pick up the same audio.
- No sound: check that the room speakers, and not your laptop speakers, are chosen in the meeting audio settings.
- Camera not found: unplug and reconnect the USB cable, then rejoin the meeting.

If none of these works, raise a ServiceHub ticket with the room name and a short description.

## §4 Large Events
For large events such as town halls, the AV team needs 5 working days notice when there are more than 100 attendees. Submit a ServiceHub request under "AV > Event Support" with the date, venue, expected attendance and whether the event is hybrid or livestreamed.

The AV team arranges extra microphones, a stage display, a streaming feed and a rehearsal slot. Late requests cannot be guaranteed technical staff or additional equipment.
