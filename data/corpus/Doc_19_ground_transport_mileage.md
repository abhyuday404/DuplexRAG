# Ground Transport and Mileage

## §1 Airport and Local Travel
Under the Ground Transport and Mileage policy, airport transfers and local travel during business trips are done by app-based cabs in the sedan category.

- Premium SUVs are permitted only when 3 or more travellers share the same vehicle.
- Employees should use the ride-sharing option where available on longer routes.
- Metro, suburban train and airport express services are encouraged for short hops in cities where they are reliable.
- Cab receipts should be attached to the SpendWise claim for any fare above INR 500 (Doc_16 Section 1).

## §2 Own Vehicle Mileage
Under the Ground Transport and Mileage policy, employees who use their own vehicle for approved business travel are reimbursed by distance.

- Car: INR 12 per km.
- Two-wheeler: INR 5 per km.
- Employees must log the start and end points and the odometer reading or map distance for every trip.

The mileage rate is intended to cover fuel, wear and routine maintenance, so fuel bills are not reimbursed separately. Parking and toll charges are reimbursed at actuals with receipts. Mileage between home and the regular place of work is not claimable.

## §3 Rental Cars
Under the Ground Transport and Mileage policy, self-drive or chauffeur-driven rental cars are permitted only with prior director approval.

- The approval should state the reason a rental is more practical than app-based cabs, for example multi-city site visits with no reliable public transport.
- Fuel receipts are required for every refill.
- Rentals should be booked through TripDesk so that the corporate rate and insurance are applied.
- Fines, challans and damage caused by misuse are the employee's responsibility and are not reimbursable.

## §4 Late-Night Travel
Under the Ground Transport and Mileage policy, travel after 10 pm is arranged by the company rather than left to the employee.

- After 10 pm, the company arranges a cab for the employee, whether for a home drop after a late shift or an airport transfer.
- Women employees get an escort as per the safety policy, and the cab and driver details are shared with the security desk before the trip starts.
- Employees who must travel late at night on business trips should request the cab from TripDesk at least 4 hours in advance.

Emergency and safety support for travellers is covered in Doc_26.
