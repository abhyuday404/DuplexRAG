# Team Offsite and Outing Guidelines

## §1 Eligibility and Budget
The Team Offsite and Outing Guidelines give each team INR 2,500 per person per financial year for outings. Offsites of up to 2 days need director approval.

- The allowance is calculated on the team's headcount at the start of the financial year.
- Unused allowance does not carry over to the next financial year.
- Offsites are not measured against the per-attendee ceilings for trainings and workshops in Doc_01.

## §2 Travel to Offsites
Travel to team offsites follows the Global Travel Policy (Doc_15).

- Group bookings go through the travel desk, not through individual employee bookings.
- Raise the group booking request as soon as the director has approved the offsite, so that fares are still competitive.
- Local transport at the offsite location is booked through the same desk.

## §3 Alcohol and Conduct
The Team Offsite and Outing Guidelines state that company funds cannot pay for alcohol, at any venue or event.

- The Code of Conduct (Doc_34) applies at all offsites and outings, including evenings and travel time.
- Managers are responsible for conduct of their teams at the event.
- Employees who pay for alcohol personally must keep it separate from the claim.

## §4 Safety
Safety at team offsites and outings is the responsibility of the organiser.

- Adventure activities need signed waivers from every participant and certified operators.
- Operator certificates should be attached to the offsite request.
- The safety rules in Doc_12 apply where the offsite is held at an external venue with more than 50 attendees.

## §5 Claims
Claims for team offsites and outings must be submitted within 15 days of the event, together with the attendee list.

- Claims are charged to the team's cost centre.
- Receipts must be itemised, and alcohol is excluded from any claim.
- Late claims need approval from the department director.
