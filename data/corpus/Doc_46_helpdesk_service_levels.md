# IT Helpdesk Service Levels

## §1 Contacting the Helpdesk
The IT helpdesk at Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. can be reached in three ways.

- ServiceHub portal: the preferred route, because every ticket is tracked and time-stamped.
- Phone: extension 4357 (HELP) from any office phone.
- Chat: the ServiceHub chat window, for quick questions and follow-ups.

When you contact the helpdesk, give your name, employee ID, location, the device or application affected, and the exact error message. Attach a screenshot where you can. Tickets that include this detail are resolved faster, because the first-line team does not have to ask again.

## §2 Priorities and SLAs
The helpdesk assigns every ticket a priority, and each priority has its own service level agreement (SLA).

- P1 business-wide outage: response 15 minutes, resolution 4 hours.
- P2: response 1 hour, resolution 1 business day.
- P3: response 8 business hours, resolution 3 business days.
- P4 service requests: 5 business days.

A P1 is reserved for events that stop the whole business, such as a network or email outage. A P2 usually affects a team, a P3 affects one person with a workaround available, and a P4 is a routine request such as a software install or a new accessory. The helpdesk sets the priority, but you may ask for a review if you think it is wrong.

## §3 Escalation
For an SLA breach on IT tickets, escalate to the IT duty manager. Do this after the response or resolution time for the priority has passed and you have had no update.

1. Open your ticket in ServiceHub and click "Escalate".
2. State the SLA that was missed and the business impact.
3. If the matter is urgent, call extension 4357 and ask for the IT duty manager.

The duty manager acknowledges the escalation and gives you a revised time. Repeated breaches are reviewed in the monthly service report.

## §4 Support Hours
The support hours of the IT helpdesk depend on priority. P1 incidents get 24x7 cover, including nights, Sundays and public holidays, and the on-call team can be reached on extension 4357. For all other priorities, support is available from 08:00 to 20:00 IST, Monday to Saturday.

SLA timers for P2, P3 and P4 tickets only run within these hours. A P3 ticket raised at 19:00 on Saturday therefore begins its 8 business hours on Monday morning. Outside these hours, raise the ticket in ServiceHub and it will be picked up at the start of the next support day.
