# Wellness Benefits

## §1 Health Insurance
Under the Wellness Benefits policy, Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. provides group health insurance to every employee from the date of joining. The cover is a family floater of INR 5,00,000, shared by the employee, spouse and up to 2 dependent children.

- A parents add-on is optional. The premium for parents is paid by the employee and deducted from salary.
- Enrolment for the add-on is open for 30 days after joining and during the annual open enrolment window in PeopleHub.
- Insurance cards and the cashless hospital network list are available in PeopleHub.

## §2 Gym and Fitness
Under the Wellness Benefits policy, employees can claim a fitness allowance for gym memberships, yoga classes, sports coaching and similar activities. Gym and fitness costs are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per month.

- Submit the receipt or membership invoice in PeopleHub by the 25th of the month.
- Annual or quarterly memberships can be claimed at INR 1,500 for each month covered.
- Fitness equipment and supplements are not eligible.

## §3 Counselling (EAP)
Under the Wellness Benefits policy, the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers professional counselling through the MindWell programme. Every employee receives 6 free confidential sessions per year via the MindWell programme.

- Sessions can be booked directly with MindWell by phone or through its app, without informing the manager or HR.
- Counselling covers stress, anxiety, relationships, financial worries and grief.
- MindWell shares only anonymous usage statistics with the company. Individual details are never disclosed.

## §4 Annual Health Check-up
Under the Wellness Benefits policy, employees receive a preventive health check-up each year. The annual health check-up is free for employees above 30.

- The package covers blood work, ECG, vision and a doctor consultation, at partner diagnostic centres.
- Book a slot through PeopleHub. Reports are shared only with the employee.
- Employees aged 30 or below can take the same package at the negotiated corporate rate, at their own cost.
