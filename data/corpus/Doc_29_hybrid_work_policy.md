# Hybrid Work Policy

## §1 Office Attendance
Under the Hybrid Work Policy, employees of Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. are expected to work from an office 3 days per week and may work from home on the remaining working days.

- The anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday. All employees should be in office on these days so that team meetings, reviews and workshops can be held in person.
- The third office day is chosen by the team and should be agreed with the manager.
- Employees on approved leave, public holidays or client travel are excluded from the weekly count.
- Roles that need lab or hardware access may have additional office days, as communicated by the department head.

## §2 Core Hours
Under the Hybrid Work Policy, core hours are the period during which every employee is expected to be available online or in office, wherever they are working. Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST.

- Meetings involving more than one team should be scheduled inside core hours.
- Outside core hours, employees can arrange their start and end times with their manager, subject to a full working day of 9 hours.

## §3 Work-From-Home Allowances
Under the Hybrid Work Policy, Halcyon Labs provides two allowances to support employees working from home.

- Internet reimbursement: INR 1,000 per month, claimed in PeopleHub with a broadband or mobile data bill.
- Home office setup allowance: a one-time amount of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk, monitor or similar items. It can be claimed once in an employee's tenure, within 6 months of joining or of starting hybrid work.

Claims must be supported by invoices and are paid with the next payroll cycle.

## §4 Working From Another City
Under the Hybrid Work Policy, employees may work remotely from a city other than their assigned office location for a limited period each year.

- Up to 30 days per year with manager approval.
- Requests are raised in PeopleHub at least 5 working days before the start date, with the address and dates.
- Working from outside India is not permitted due to tax and data rules. This includes short stays, workations and remote work during personal travel abroad.
- Employees must remain reachable during core hours and follow information security guidelines on any network they use.
