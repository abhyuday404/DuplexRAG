# Holiday Calendar 2026 (India Offices)

## §1 National Holidays
Under the Holiday Calendar 2026, all India offices of Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. are closed on three national holidays.

- Republic Day: 26 January
- Independence Day: 15 August
- Gandhi Jayanti: 2 October

These holidays apply to every employee regardless of location. Employees on call for production support are compensated with a compensatory off, as described in the on-call guidelines.

## §2 Festival Holidays
Under the Holiday Calendar 2026, the company observes five festival holidays across all India offices.

- Holi: 4 March
- Eid al-Fitr: 21 March
- Good Friday: 3 April
- Diwali: 8 November
- Christmas: 25 December

Festival dates follow the published calendar for the year. If an official date changes because of moon sighting, HR will announce the revised date in PeopleHub at least 2 days ahead.

## §3 Floating Holidays
Under the Holiday Calendar 2026, each employee may choose 2 floating holidays per year for festivals or occasions that are not on the fixed list.

- Choose the floating holidays from the published list in PeopleHub by 31 January.
- Floating holidays must be approved by the manager and are subject to business needs.
- Unselected floating holidays lapse and cannot be carried forward or encashed.
- Employees who join after 31 January choose their floating holidays within 15 days of joining, in proportion to the remaining months.

## §4 Location-Specific Holidays
Under the Holiday Calendar 2026, some offices observe one additional holiday that is specific to the state or city.

- Pune: Ganesh Chaturthi on 14 September
- Bengaluru: Karnataka Rajyotsava on 1 November
- Chennai: Pongal on 15 January
- Hyderabad: Telangana Formation Day on 2 June
- Noida: no additional holiday

Employees working from a different office on a holiday follow the calendar of their assigned base location.
