# Corporate Card Policy

## §1 Eligibility
Under the Corporate Card Policy, a corporate credit card is issued to employees who travel 4 or more times a year for business.

- The credit limit is INR 2,00,000 per card.
- Requests are raised on PeopleHub with line manager approval and are processed by Finance within 10 working days.
- The card is issued in the employee's name, and the employee is the sole authorised user.
- Cards are reviewed each April, and cards of employees who no longer meet the travel threshold may be withdrawn.

## §2 Permitted Use
Under the Corporate Card Policy, the corporate card may be used only for business travel and approved client entertainment.

- Permitted: airfare, hotels, local transport, and meals with clients within the limits of Doc_25.
- Personal spends are prohibited, even if the employee intends to repay them.
- Cash withdrawals on the card are not permitted.
- Expenses that are non-reimbursable under Doc_16 Section 3, such as alcohol and minibar charges, must not be put on the card.

Any personal charge found on a statement is recovered from the employee's salary, and repeated misuse can lead to card withdrawal and disciplinary action.

## §3 Reconciliation
Under the Corporate Card Policy, card transactions must be reconciled in SpendWise within a fixed time after each statement.

- Reconcile all transactions in SpendWise within 10 days of the statement date.
- Each transaction must be matched to a trip report with a receipt attached.
- Cards that remain unreconciled after 30 days are suspended until the outstanding items are cleared.

Suspended cards are reactivated within 2 working days of Finance confirming that the reconciliation is complete. Reconciliation follows the same claim process described in Doc_22.

## §4 Lost Card
Under the Corporate Card Policy, a lost or stolen corporate card must be reported without delay.

- Report the loss within 24 hours to both the issuing bank and Finance.
- The bank blocks the card immediately, and a replacement is issued in about 7 working days.
- Employees are not liable for fraudulent transactions made after the loss is reported, provided the report was made within 24 hours.
- Transactions made before the report remain the employee's responsibility until the bank completes its investigation.

Employees travelling abroad should call the international number printed on the back of the card and then inform Finance by email.
