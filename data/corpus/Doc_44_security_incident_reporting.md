# Security Incident Reporting

## §1 What to Report
Security incident reporting at Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. covers anything that could put company or customer data at risk. Report all of the following, even if you are unsure and even if you think you caused the problem yourself:

- Phishing emails, messages or phone calls that ask for credentials, payments or personal data.
- Malware, such as unexpected pop-ups, ransomware notes or antivirus alerts you cannot clear.
- Lost or stolen devices, including laptops, phones and security tokens.
- Suspected data leaks, for example a file sent to the wrong recipient or shared publicly by mistake.
- Unauthorised access, including unknown sign-ins to your account or access to systems you should not see.

## §2 How to Report
To report a security incident, contact the Security team within 1 hour of noticing it. Send an email to security@halcyonlabs.example with a short description, the time it happened, the devices or accounts involved and any screenshots.

For suspicious email, use the "Report Phish" button in your mail client. The button removes the message from your inbox and sends a copy, with full headers, to the Security team. Do not forward the message as an attachment, and do not click links or open attachments to check whether they are genuine. Out of hours, call extension 4357 and ask for the security on-call engineer.

## §3 After Reporting
After you report an incident, preserve the evidence and follow the guidance from the Security team.

- Do not delete evidence such as emails, files, logs or the affected device state.
- If malware is suspected, disconnect from the network by turning off Wi-Fi and unplugging the network cable, but keep the device powered on.
- Do not try to clean or reinstall the device yourself.
- Change your password from a different, trusted device when advised.
- Keep the incident details confidential until the Security team confirms who should be told.

The Security team sends a ServiceHub incident number and updates you until the case is closed.

## §4 Data Breaches
For a confirmed or suspected data breach, the Security team escalates the incident to the Data Protection Officer, who is notified as soon as personal data may be involved. The Data Protection Officer decides whether the breach must be disclosed and coordinates the response with Legal.

Regulator notification is made within 72 hours where required, counted from when the company becomes aware of the breach. Affected customers or employees are informed when the assessment shows a risk to them. Reporting within 1 hour makes this deadline achievable.
