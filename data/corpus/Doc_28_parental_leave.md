# Parental Leave

## §1 Maternity Leave
Under the Parental Leave policy, maternity leave is paid leave granted to an eligible employee at Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. for childbirth and recovery. The entitlement depends on the number of children.

- 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children.
- 12 weeks of paid leave for the third child onwards.
- Leave may begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.
- Apply in PeopleHub with a medical certificate of the expected delivery date, ideally at least 30 days before the leave begins.

## §2 Paternity Leave
Under the Parental Leave policy, paternity leave is paid leave available to an employee who becomes a father, including through surrogacy.

- 4 weeks of paid leave.
- It must be taken within 6 months of birth.
- It can be split into two blocks, for example 2 weeks around the birth and 2 weeks later.
- Apply in PeopleHub and share the birth certificate or hospital discharge summary after the child is born.

## §3 Adoption Leave
Under the Parental Leave policy, adoption leave is paid leave for an employee who legally adopts a child. Adoption leave is 12 weeks paid for the primary caregiver.

- Only one parent per adoption can claim leave as the primary caregiver. The other parent may use paternity leave where eligible.
- Leave starts from the date the child is placed with the family.
- Submit the adoption order or equivalent legal documents to HR through PeopleHub.

## §4 Return to Work Support
Under the Parental Leave policy, Halcyon Labs supports employees returning from maternity, paternity or adoption leave with a phased return and childcare assistance.

- Phased return: 4 weeks at 60% hours, with pay and benefits unchanged. The schedule is agreed with the manager before the return date.
- Creche reimbursement: INR 8,000 per month until the child turns 5. Claims are submitted monthly in PeopleHub with the creche receipt.
- Employees may request a check-in with their HR business partner in the first month after returning to discuss workload and flexibility.
