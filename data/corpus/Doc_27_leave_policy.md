# Leave Policy

## §1 Leave Types and Entitlement
Under the Leave Policy, every confirmed full-time employee of Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. is entitled to three types of paid leave in each calendar year. Balances are visible in PeopleHub at all times.

- Earned leave: 18 days per year, accrued at 1.5 days per month. Accrual starts from the date of joining and is pro-rated for part months.
- Sick leave: 10 days per year, credited at the start of the year.
- Casual leave: 6 days per year, meant for short personal or unplanned needs.


## §2 Applying for Leave
Under the Leave Policy, all leave requests must be raised in PeopleHub and routed to the reporting manager for approval. Requests sent only by email or chat are not recorded for payroll.

- Earned leave longer than 3 days must be applied for at least 7 days in advance.
- Casual leave and sick leave can be applied on the same day, ideally before 10:00 IST, with a short intimation to the manager.

## §3 Carry-Forward and Encashment
Under the Leave Policy, unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year, subject to a cap on the total balance. Casual leave and sick leave do not carry forward and lapse on 31 December.

- Earned leave carry-forward is capped at 30 days. Any balance above 30 days lapses at year end.
- Up to 10 days of earned leave per year can be encashed at basic pay.

## §4 Sick Leave Documentation
Under the Leave Policy, sick leave of up to 2 consecutive days does not require any supporting document. A medical certificate is needed for more than 2 consecutive sick days.

- The certificate must be issued by a registered medical practitioner and state the dates of illness.
- Upload the certificate in PeopleHub within 3 working days of returning to work.

## §5 Leave During Notice Period
Under the Leave Policy, an employee who has resigned may apply for leave during the notice period, but earned leave during notice needs manager approval. Approval depends on handover status and project commitments.

- Leave taken during notice does not shorten or extend the notice period unless HR agrees in writing.
- Unused earned leave at the last working day is settled as part of full and final settlement, within the 10-day encashment limit described above.
