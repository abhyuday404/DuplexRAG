# OMR Knowledge Centre, Chennai - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
OMR Knowledge Centre is a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02) on Old Mahabalipuram Road, in Sholinganallur, Chennai. It sits in the IT corridor where many Chennai-based Halcyon customers have offices, which makes it convenient for customer workshops and partner trainings.

- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount applies to published rates.
- Bookings go through the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example.
- Organisers should read the Monsoon Advisory in section 5 before planning any event between October and December.

## §2 Capacity
OMR Knowledge Centre has two halls.

- Hall 1: 70 theatre or 45 classroom.
- Hall 2: 25 classroom.

Hall 2 is the usual choice for small technical trainings and for a second track running alongside a Hall 1 event.

## §3 Cancellation Policy
The cancellation policy at OMR Knowledge Centre is tiered, and it moves quickly in the last week.

- Free cancellation until 7 days before the event.
- 30% charge if cancelled 2-6 days before.
- 100% charge within 48 hours.

Charges are borne by the organising department's cost centre under Doc_01.

## §4 Catering
OMR Knowledge Centre provides in-house catering, and external caterers are not allowed.

- South Indian vegetarian menu: INR 700 per head.
- Mixed menu: INR 850 per head.

Because external caterers are not permitted here, the Chennai vendor listed in Doc_11 (Kaveri Meals) cannot be used at this venue.

## §5 Monsoon Advisory
The monsoon advisory for OMR Knowledge Centre applies to events planned between October and December, when heavy rain can affect travel along Old Mahabalipuram Road.

- For October to December events, plan a backup date and share it with attendees early.
- If the venue cancels due to flooding, the booking is fully refunded.
- Organisers should confirm travel arrangements for outstation attendees with a flexible fare where possible.
