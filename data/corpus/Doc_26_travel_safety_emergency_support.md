# Travel Safety and Emergency Support

## §1 24x7 Helpline
Under the Travel Safety and Emergency Support policy, employees on business travel have access to the Halcyon SafeTravel helpline at +91-80-5555-0100.

- The helpline is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays.
- It handles medical, security, natural disaster and travel-disruption emergencies, in India and abroad.
- Employees should save the number in their phone before departure and share it with a family member.
- Calls are logged, and a case owner from the Security team follows up until the situation is resolved.

## §2 High-Risk Destinations
Under the Travel Safety and Emergency Support policy, travel to destinations on the company's high-risk list needs security clearance in advance.

- Security approval must be obtained at least 10 days before travel.
- The traveller must do a daily check-in with the Security team while travelling.
- The high-risk list is reviewed monthly and is published on PeopleHub.
- Travel is cancelled or recalled if the situation on the ground worsens.

The approval is in addition to the director approval required for international travel in Doc_15 Section 6.

## §3 Medical Emergencies Abroad
Under the Travel Safety and Emergency Support policy, employees who need medical care abroad should first call the insurance helpline printed on the group policy card.

- Use network hospitals wherever possible so that treatment can be cashless.
- Inform the SafeTravel helpline as soon as it is safe to do so.
- Medical cover under group policy HLI-GTI-2026 is up to USD 100,000, as described in Doc_20.
- Keep all hospital bills and prescriptions, and photograph them in SpendWise.

If a non-network hospital is used in an emergency, the employee pays first and claims reimbursement from the insurer with the Risk team's help.

## §4 Lost Passport
Under the Travel Safety and Emergency Support policy, an employee who loses a passport abroad should act immediately.

- Contact the nearest Indian embassy or consulate and file a police report.
- Inform the SafeTravel helpline and the line manager.
- TripDesk arranges emergency re-booking of flights and hotels once an emergency travel document is issued.
- Costs of the emergency certificate and re-booking are reimbursed if the loss was not due to negligence.

Employees should carry photocopies of the passport and visa, stored separately from the originals.
