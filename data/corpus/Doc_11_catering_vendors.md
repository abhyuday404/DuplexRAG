# Approved Catering Vendors

## §1 How the Approved List Works
The Halcyon Labs Approved Catering Vendors list names the external caterers that organisers may use for Halcyon events. Vendors must hold an FSSAI licence and a hygiene certificate to be on the list, and both documents are re-checked by the Events Desk every year.

- Use external vendors only where the venue permits external catering. Several preferred venues allow in-house catering only.
- Where a venue has its own catering, the venue fact sheet sets the rates.
- Rates below are per head and are indicative; the vendor confirms the final quote in writing.

## §2 Pune Vendors
Halcyon Labs has two approved catering vendors in Pune.

- Annapurna Caterers: minimum order 25 plates, vegetarian INR 650 per head, Jain menu available.
- GreenLeaf Kitchens: minimum 15 plates, vegan and gluten-free options, INR 780 per head.

Organisers who need vegan or gluten-free menus should use GreenLeaf Kitchens; those who need Jain menus should use Annapurna Caterers.

## §3 Bengaluru Vendors
Halcyon Labs has two approved catering vendors in Bengaluru.

- Tiffin Trail: INR 1,050 per head, minimum 20.
- Malnad Kitchens: INR 1,150 per head, minimum 30.

Both are partner caterers of Cubbon Heritage Hall (Doc_07), which allows catering only through these two vendors. They may also be used at Whitefield Innovation Hub (Doc_06), subject to its INR 5,000 kitchen usage fee.

## §4 Hyderabad and Chennai Vendors
Halcyon Labs has one approved catering vendor in each of Hyderabad and Chennai.

- Deccan Pot (Hyderabad): INR 900 per head.
- Kaveri Meals (Chennai): INR 700 per head.

Before booking either vendor, confirm that the venue allows external catering. Some Hyderabad and Chennai venues in Doc_02 serve only their own menus.

## §5 Ordering and Payment
The ordering and payment rules for approved catering vendors apply to every order, whatever its size.

- Order at least 5 working days ahead of the event.
- Invoices are billed to the event cost centre.
- No cash payments are made to vendors.
- Send final headcount and dietary needs (Jain, vegan, allergies) to the vendor at the time of ordering, and follow the dietary rules in Doc_01.
