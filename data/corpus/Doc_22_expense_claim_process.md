# Expense Claim Process (SpendWise)

## §1 Submitting a Claim
Under the Expense Claim Process, all travel expenses are claimed by creating a trip report in the SpendWise portal or mobile app.

- Create one trip report per trip and add each expense as a line item.
- Attach a receipt for every expense above INR 500, as required by Doc_16.
- Submit the report within 15 days of trip end.
- Link the TripDesk booking reference so that fares and hotel rates are checked against the benchmark automatically.

Draft reports can be saved and completed later, but the 15-day deadline runs from the trip end date and not from the date the draft was created.

## §2 Approval Workflow
Under the Expense Claim Process, every submitted claim passes through a two-stage approval before payment.

- The line manager approves the business purpose and the amounts.
- Finance then audits the claim against the policy and the receipts.
- Claims above INR 1,00,000 need director approval as a second level, before the Finance audit.

Approvers are expected to act within 3 working days. Claims that are pending with an approver for more than 5 working days are escalated automatically to the approver's manager.

## §3 Missing Receipts
Under the Expense Claim Process, an employee who has lost a receipt may submit a missing-receipt declaration in SpendWise.

- A missing-receipt declaration is allowed up to INR 2,000 per trip.
- It can be used at most twice per year per employee.
- The declaration must state the date, vendor, amount and the reason the receipt is unavailable.

Declarations do not apply to foreign-currency expenses, which always need an original receipt under Doc_16 Section 4. Hotel invoices and airline tickets can be retrieved from TripDesk and are not eligible for a declaration.

## §4 Payment Timelines
Under the Expense Claim Process, approved claims are paid in the next payroll cycle or within 30 days of approval, whichever is earlier.

- Payment is made to the salary bank account registered on PeopleHub.
- The payment advice is emailed and appears under the claim in SpendWise.
- Corporate card spends are settled directly with the bank and are not paid to the employee (see Doc_21).

## §5 Audits and Returned Claims
Under the Expense Claim Process, 10% of all claims are randomly audited by Finance each month, in addition to the standard audit in the approval workflow.

- Audited claims may require the original receipts to be produced on request.
- A claim returned by an approver or by Finance must be corrected and resubmitted within 7 days.
- Claims not resubmitted within 7 days are closed, and a fresh claim would fall under the late-claim rules in Doc_16 Section 5.
