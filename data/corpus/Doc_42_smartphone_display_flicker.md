# Smartphone Troubleshooting - Display Flicker

## §1 Quick Checks
For smartphone display flicker, begin with two quick checks. Flicker at low brightness usually comes from adaptive brightness, which changes the backlight level in small steps as the light sensor reacts. To find out whether an installed app is the cause, boot into Safe Mode.

1. Hold the power button and touch and hold "Power off".
2. Tap OK when the Safe Mode prompt appears.
3. Use the phone for a few minutes with third-party apps disabled and watch the screen at low and high brightness.

If the flicker goes away in Safe Mode, a downloaded app is responsible. Remove recently installed apps one by one.

## §2 Settings Fixes
For display flicker that remains after the quick checks, change these display settings.

- Go to Settings > Display > turn off Adaptive brightness.
- Set the refresh rate to Standard (60 Hz) under Settings > Display > Refresh rate.
- Disable the Eye comfort schedule under Settings > Display > Eye comfort.
- In Developer options, disable HW overlays. Enable Developer options first by tapping Build number 7 times in Settings > About phone.

After each change, keep the phone at a low brightness for a minute to see if the flicker has stopped.

## §3 Software Fixes
For display flicker caused by software, update the phone and clear cached data.

- Install the latest OS update from Settings > System > Software update, and connect to Wi-Fi with at least 50% battery first.
- Clear the cache partition: switch the phone off, hold Power and Volume Up to enter recovery mode, choose "Wipe cache partition" and confirm. This does not delete personal files or apps.
- Update the apps that draw on the screen at the time of flicker, such as the launcher and the browser.

Back up your data before any recovery-mode step.

## §4 Hardware Escalation
For display flicker that is a hardware fault, escalate to IT once the software steps have failed. If flicker persists in Safe Mode it is hardware, and the panel or its connector needs to be inspected. Raise a ServiceHub device ticket and include the phone model, asset tag and a short video of the flicker.

A loaner phone is provided within 1 working day for company devices, so you can carry on working while yours is repaired. Move your work apps to the loaner using the MDM enrolment steps. Personal phones enrolled in BYOD are not repaired by IT, and you should contact your device manufacturer.
