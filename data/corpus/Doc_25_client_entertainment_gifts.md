# Client Entertainment and Gifts

## §1 Principles
Under the Client Entertainment and Gifts policy, all hospitality and gifts must comply with the company's anti-bribery commitments and the applicable anti-corruption laws.

- Entertainment and gifts must never be offered or accepted to influence a business decision.
- They must be reasonable in value, infrequent, transparent and appropriate to the business relationship.
- Nothing may be offered while a tender, contract negotiation or regulatory decision involving the recipient is pending.

Employees who are unsure whether an offer is appropriate should ask the Compliance team before proceeding.

## §2 Entertainment Limits
Under the Client Entertainment and Gifts policy, spending on client entertainment is limited per person and per event.

- The limit is INR 5,000 per client attendee per event.
- Pre-approval is needed when the total cost of an event is above INR 50,000.
- Pre-approval is given by the department director and recorded in SpendWise before the event.

Employee meals at the same event are counted within the total but are not subject to the per-attendee limit. The corporate card may be used for approved client entertainment as per Doc_21. Per diem is not payable for a meal that is hosted by the employee for a client (Doc_17).

## §3 Gifts
Under the Client Entertainment and Gifts policy, gifts to clients are allowed only within strict limits.

- The value of a gift to a client must not exceed INR 3,000.
- Cash and gift cards, including vouchers that can be exchanged for cash, are never permitted.
- No gifts may be given to government officials or employees of public sector bodies, under any circumstances.
- Company-branded items of nominal value, such as diaries and pens, may be given at events.

Gifts received by employees above INR 3,000 in value must be declined or handed over to Compliance.

## §4 Recording
Under the Client Entertainment and Gifts policy, every gift and every entertainment event must be recorded in the gifts register.

- Record the entry in the gifts register within 7 days of the event or the gift.
- The entry must show the date, the recipient's name and organisation, the value and the business purpose.
- Compliance reviews the register every quarter and reports exceptions to the Audit Committee.

Failure to record an entry within 7 days is treated as a policy breach, and the related expense may be rejected at audit under Doc_22.
