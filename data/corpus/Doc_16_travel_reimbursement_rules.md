# Travel Reimbursement Rules

## §1 Standard Reimbursement Rule
Under the Travel Reimbursement Rules, business travel expenses are reimbursed at actuals within the approved limits set in the travel policies.

- Claims must be submitted within 15 days of trip completion.
- Itemised receipts are required for every expense above INR 500.
- Approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle or within 30 days, whichever is earlier.

Claims are raised in SpendWise; the step-by-step process is in Doc_22. Expenses that are covered by a fixed allowance, such as per diem, do not need receipts.

## §2 Eligible Expenses
Under the Travel Reimbursement Rules, the following expenses are eligible for reimbursement when incurred on approved business travel.

- Airfare and train fares booked as per the Global Travel Policy (Doc_15).
- Hotel accommodation within the caps in Doc_18.
- Per diem for meals and incidentals as per Doc_17.
- Local transport as per Doc_19.
- Visa fees and travel insurance.
- Business calls and internet charges up to INR 1,000 per trip.

## §3 Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Under the Travel Reimbursement Rules, the following expenses are never reimbursed, even if a receipt is available.

- Alcohol and minibar charges.
- Personal entertainment such as movies, spa and sightseeing tours.
- Traffic fines and parking penalties.
- Laundry on trips shorter than 4 nights.
- Unapproved upgrades of flight class, hotel room category or rental vehicle.

Employees who include such items in a claim will have them deducted by Finance at audit, and repeated inclusion is escalated to the line manager.

## §4 International Travel Reimbursement
Under the Travel Reimbursement Rules, foreign currency receipt verification is mandatory for all international trips.

- Every foreign-currency expense needs an original receipt showing the amount and the currency.
- Finance verifies each receipt and converts the amount at the forex card rate.
- For spends made by card or cash outside the forex card, Finance converts at the RBI reference rate on the date of the expense.
- Without verifiable foreign-currency receipts, incidentals are capped at USD 25 per day.

Receipts should be photographed in the SpendWise app while travelling, and the originals retained until the claim is paid. Further guidance on forex is in Doc_20.

## §5 Late Bookings and Late Claims
Under the Travel Reimbursement Rules, expenses from bookings made after the travel took place are reimbursed only with senior director approval, as set out in Doc_15 Section 5.

- Such bookings are reimbursed at the lower of the actual fare or the TripDesk benchmark fare.
- Claims submitted more than 60 days after trip completion are rejected unless the CFO grants an exception.
- Claims submitted between 16 and 60 days after trip completion are accepted but flagged for Finance review and reported to the department head.

Employees should request a CFO exception in writing through PeopleHub, with the reason for the delay.
