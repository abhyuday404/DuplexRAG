# Onboarding Guide

## §1 Before Day One
Under the Onboarding Guide, every new joiner at Halcyon Labs India Pvt. Ltd. must complete a short set of pre-joining formalities so that accounts, payroll and insurance are ready on the first day.

- Submit the following in PeopleHub: ID proof, address proof, education certificates and bank details.
- Upload documents at least 3 working days before the joining date.
- Original documents may be verified by HR on the first day.
- Relieving and experience letters from the previous employer are due within 30 days of joining.

## §2 Day One Checklist
Under the Onboarding Guide, the first day follows a fixed checklist so that new joiners are productive quickly.

- Collect the laptop from the IT desk. Bring a government photo ID.
- Get the ID badge from the front desk or security.
- Induction starts at 10:00 in the training room. Please arrive by 09:45.
- Complete the information security and Code of Conduct acknowledgements in PeopleHub by the end of the day.
- Meet your manager for a walk-through of the first-week plan.

## §3 Buddy Programme
Under the Onboarding Guide, the Buddy Programme pairs each new joiner with an experienced colleague who helps them settle in. Every new joiner gets a buddy for the first 30 days.

- The buddy is from the same or an adjacent team and is not the reporting manager.
- Buddies answer day-to-day questions on tools, processes, cafeteria and office etiquette.
- The buddy and new joiner meet informally at least once a week during the 30 days.
- HR shares the buddy's name and contact details before day one.

## §4 Probation and First 90 Days
Under the Onboarding Guide, all new employees serve a probation period before confirmation. Probation is 6 months, with a check-in review at 90 days.

- The 90-day review is a structured conversation with the manager about goals, feedback and any support needed.
- The manager records the outcome in PeopleHub.
- Confirmation is decided at the end of month 6. In some cases probation may be extended by up to 3 months, with written notice.
- Leave entitlements accrue during probation as described in the Leave Policy.
