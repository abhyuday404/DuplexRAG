# Whitefield Innovation Hub, Bengaluru - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
Whitefield Innovation Hub is a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02) in Whitefield, Bengaluru, near ITPL. It is within walking distance of the Purple Line metro, which makes it convenient for attendees who prefer not to drive through Whitefield traffic.

- It is the largest preferred venue in Bengaluru and suits all-hands sessions, product launches and customer conferences.
- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount applies to published rates.
- Bookings go through the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example.

## §2 Capacity
Whitefield Innovation Hub has one large auditorium and two studios.

- Auditorium: 120 theatre.
- Studio 1: 30 classroom.
- Studio 2: 30 classroom.

The two studios are separate rooms and cannot be combined. For a workshop of more than 30 people that needs breakout groups, the usual pattern is to use the Auditorium for plenary sessions and the two studios for parallel tracks.

## §3 Cancellation Policy
Whitefield Innovation Hub's cancellation policy is based on the days remaining before the event.

- Free cancellation until 10 days before the event.
- 40% charge if cancelled 3-9 days before.
- 100% charge if cancelled less than 3 days before.

Charges are borne by the organising department's cost centre under Doc_01.

## §4 Catering
Whitefield Innovation Hub offers in-house catering and also permits approved external caterers.

- In-house vegetarian: INR 800 per head.
- In-house non-vegetarian: INR 1,000 per head.
- Approved external caterers from Doc_11 are allowed, with an INR 5,000 kitchen usage fee.

External caterers must be on the Halcyon approved list; unlisted caterers are not permitted, even if the venue would accept them.

## §5 AV
Whitefield Innovation Hub has a strong AV set-up for hybrid and broadcast events.

- Livestream studio for broadcasting sessions to remote attendees.
- 8 microphones across the Auditorium and studios.
- Recording is available on request; ask the Events Desk when booking.

Recordings of customer sessions should be shared only with attendee consent, as described in Doc_13.
