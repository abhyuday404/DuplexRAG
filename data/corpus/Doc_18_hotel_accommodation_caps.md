# Hotel Accommodation Caps

## §1 General Rules
Under the Hotel Accommodation Caps policy, hotel stays for business travel follow a small set of general rules that apply in every city.

- Hotels must be booked through TripDesk, which shows only properties that meet the company's safety and hygiene standards.
- Employees should stay close to the work location or client site to keep local transport costs low.
- Room sharing is not mandatory; every traveller is entitled to a single room within the cap.
- Room service, minibar and laundry are subject to the exclusions in Doc_16 Section 3.

## §2 Domestic Caps per Night
Under the Hotel Accommodation Caps policy, the following nightly caps apply to domestic stays, excluding taxes.

- Mumbai: INR 9,000.
- Delhi NCR: INR 8,500.
- Bengaluru: INR 7,500.
- Pune: INR 6,500.
- Hyderabad: INR 7,000.
- Chennai: INR 6,800.
- All other cities: INR 5,000.

These caps are for the room rate only. GST and other statutory taxes are reimbursed in addition to the cap, and TripDesk displays the tax-exclusive rate to make the comparison easy.

## §3 International Caps per Night
Under the Hotel Accommodation Caps policy, the following nightly caps apply to international stays, excluding taxes.

- USA: USD 220.
- UK: GBP 180.
- Germany: EUR 160.
- Singapore: SGD 280.
- Japan: JPY 25,000.

For countries not listed, TripDesk publishes a benchmark cap for each destination city, and the traveller should book within that benchmark. Hotel invoices are subject to foreign currency receipt verification (Doc_16 Section 4).

## §4 Exceeding the Cap
Under the Hotel Accommodation Caps policy, a stay may exceed the nightly cap only with the approvals described here.

- Up to 15% above the cap is allowed with line manager approval during peak events such as large conferences, trade fairs and festival weeks.
- Anything more than 15% above the cap needs director approval.
- Approval must be obtained before booking and attached to the TripDesk booking or the SpendWise claim.

Amounts above the approved limit are borne by the employee and are deducted by Finance from the claim.

## §5 Long Stays
Under the Hotel Accommodation Caps policy, stays longer than 14 nights in the same city move from a hotel to a serviced apartment arranged through TripDesk.

- The serviced apartment is booked for the full duration of the assignment.
- The apartment rate should stay within the applicable nightly cap for that city.
- Per diem continues to be paid as per Doc_17 while the employee is on the assignment.

Employees relocating permanently should refer to the Relocation Assistance policy (Doc_24) instead.
