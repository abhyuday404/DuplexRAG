# The Banyan Court, Koregaon Park, Pune - Venue Fact Sheet

## §1 Overview
The Banyan Court is a boutique heritage hotel in Koregaon Park, Pune, and a Halcyon Labs preferred venue (see Doc_02). It is best suited to small leadership meetings, customer dinners and partner briefings, especially where outstation attendees need to stay overnight.

- 18 guest rooms are available at a corporate rate of INR 6,200 per night for outstation attendees.
- The 10% Halcyon corporate discount applies to published event rates.
- Room blocks should be requested through the Events Desk at events@halcyonlabs.example along with the event booking.

## §2 Capacity
The Banyan Court has two event spaces.

- Banyan Room: 20 boardroom.
- Courtyard Banquet: 45 banquet or 35 classroom.

The Courtyard is the larger of the two and is the only space that can host the full 45-person banquet layout. Events above 45 attendees cannot be held at this venue.

## §3 Cancellation Policy
The Banyan Court's cancellation policy has four tiers based on the number of days before the event.

- Free cancellation up to 30 days before.
- 25% charge at 15-29 days before.
- 60% charge at 7-14 days before.
- 100% charge within 7 days.

Because the free window is 30 days, the booking should be confirmed only after the event has final approval under Doc_01.

## §4 Catering
The Banyan Court provides in-house catering only, and outside catering is not allowed.

- In-house buffet: INR 1,400 per head, with a minimum of 20 guests.
- High-tea package: INR 450 per head.
- Bar service needs a separate licence fee. Company funds cannot be used for alcohol under Doc_14, so bar service is not claimable against a Halcyon cost centre.

## §5 Accessibility
Accessibility at The Banyan Court varies by room, so organisers must choose the space carefully.

- The Courtyard is ground level and wheelchair accessible.
- The Banyan Room is on the first floor with no lift and is NOT wheelchair accessible.

Customer events must use wheelchair-accessible rooms (Doc_01), so any customer event held here must use the Courtyard rather than the Banyan Room.
